Porsche performance showdown

Cayman S, Macan S, Cayenne S and Cayenne Turbo (all 2015) against the 2020 Panamera Turbo S E-Hybrid. Metric units, Sport Chrono fitted on all five.

At a glance

Performance web

Each axis is scored against the best car (best = 100; centre = 0). Lightness is lowest weight, acceleration is quickest 0–100 km/h. Handling is my qualitative estimate (100 / 80 / 70 / 72 / 85), not a measured value.

Verdict

  • Best raw performance: Panamera Turbo S E-Hybrid. Most power (500 kW / 680 PS), most torque (850 Nm), 3.4 s to 100 km/h and 310 km/h top speed. It is also the best power-to-weight of the five.
  • Best handling and lightness: Cayman S. It weighs about 1,000 kg less than the Panamera, has the lowest centre of gravity and still reaches 100 km/h in 4.2 s, quicker than the Cayenne Turbo.
  • Cayenne Turbo: the strongest 2015 SUV, with 520 PS, 750 Nm, 4.4 s and 279 km/h.
  • Cayenne S and Macan S: the slowest here. The Macan S is the best balance of sporty and practical.
  • Note: the Panamera is five model years newer and a plug-in hybrid, so it is not a like-for-like rival of the 2015 cars.
