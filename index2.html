Performance web

Each axis is scored against the best car (best = 100; centre = 0). Lightness is lowest weight, acceleration is quickest 0–100 km/h. Handling is my qualitative estimate (100 / 80 / 70), not a measured value.

Verdict: the Cayman S is the best performer

  • Acceleration: quickest to 100 km/h despite the least power, because it weighs 480 to 730 kg less than the SUVs.
  • Handling: the mid-engine layout and low centre of gravity give better cornering, braking and lap times.
  • Top speed: fastest of the three at about 277 km/h.
  • Cayenne S: most power and torque, so it is strongest in rolling acceleration and towing, but it is the heaviest.
  • Macan S: the best balance of sporty and practical, but behind the Cayman S in every performance measure.
